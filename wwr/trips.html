Family Float (Beginner)

A relaxed, scenic trip designed for first-timers and families. Learn the basics of paddling, enjoy calm stretches, and finish with gentle rapids—perfect for building confidence on the river.

Duration: Half Day · Difficulty: I–II

Canyon Run (Intermediate)

Our most popular adventure—bigger waves, tighter turns, and an epic canyon view. Great for adventurous beginners and intermediate rafters looking for an exciting challenge with expert guides.

Duration: Full Day · Difficulty: III

Devil’s Teeth Expedition (Advanced)

High-adrenaline whitewater for experienced rafters. Expect technical rapids, powerful currents, and intense teamwork. This is the trip for thrill-seekers who want the river at its wildest.

Duration: 2 Days · Difficulty: IV–V
